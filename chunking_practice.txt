# Project Overview & Lore

## Introduction to the Realm
The continent of Aethelgard is divided into three distinct kingdoms, each bound by ancient magic. The air hums with residual energy from the First Cataclysm.

## Faction Dynamics
The Iron Syndicate controls all trade routes across the obsidian desert. They value profit above law.

### Syndicate Leadership
Master Kaelen runs the southern ports with an iron fist. He wears a heavy bronze gauntlet.

## Relics of Power
### The Obsidian Scepter
Found deep within the sunken vaults, the obsidian scepter grants its wielder absolute command over shadow beasts.

### The Sunstone Crown
Radiating intense heat, the sunstone crown can illuminate the darkest caverns but slowly burns the wearer's vitality.
