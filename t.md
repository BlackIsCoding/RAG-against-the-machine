The story follows Guts, a mercenary known as the Black Swordsman, wielding a massive greatsword called the Dragonslayer.

## Golden Age Arc

The Golden Age arc explores Guts's early life, his time with the Band of the Hawk led by Griffith, and the tragic events of the Eclipse.

### Griffith's Ambition

Griffith is a charismatic and ruthless leader whose dream is to rule his own kingdom. His ambition ultimately leads him to sacrifice his comrades for transcendent power, transforming into Femto, one of the God Hand.
